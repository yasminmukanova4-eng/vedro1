## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a surreal, playful-gothic confectionary atmosphere tailored for the modern Kazakhstan youth and millennial demographic. It blends folkloric dread with the electric thrill of a carnival candy shop: ominous, hyper-saturated, and indulgent. The visual tone departs from sanitized, commercial Halloween tropes to embrace an eerie neon-nocturnal world where midnight treats pulse with radioactive vitality.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast Acid Modernism**. Deep obsidian and blackened-coal base layers set an abyss-like backdrop, allowing high-voltage spectral pigments (radioactive acid green, molten pumpkin, and witching-hour violet) to leap off the glass. Surfaces feel like dark vitrines: smoked, semi-translucent sheets dusted with supernatural phosphorescence. The interface balances high-intensity thematic immersion with crisp, friction-free commerce optimized for Russian Cyrillic typography and mobile-first shopping rhythms.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for dark OLED panels, juxtaposing pitch blacks with luminescent chemical tones.

- **Primary Accent (`#FF6B00` - Pumpkin Core):** Reserved for high-intent conversion points, primary callouts, price points, and active cart operations. It evokes glowing jack-o'-lantern embers against the dark steppe sky.
- **Secondary Accent (`#39FF14` - Acid Slime):** Used for micro-badges, stock urgency notices ("В наличии", "Лимитированная серия"), sour ratings, and energetic micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#8A2BE2` - Spectral Violet):** Powers mystical overlays, backdrop lighting, subtle containment strokes, and category tags denoting dark chocolate, licorice, and enchanted assortments.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas Deep Black`: `#0D0D0D`
  - `Card Void / Elevated Surface`: `#141414`
  - `Hover / Sheet Surface`: `#1A1A1A`
  - `Ghost Border Dark`: `#2D1B4E`
  - `Primary Text (Bone White)`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `Secondary Text (Ash Mist)`: `#A0A0A0`
  - `Ghost Text (Crypt Shadow)`: `#636363`

Special state treatments use low-opacity tinted overlays: borders incorporate micro-glows using `#FF6B0033` (warm fire hazard) and `#39FF1422` (radioactive ooze) to define boundaries without breaking immersion.

## Typography

The type system balances theatrical horror with absolute catalog legibility. 

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Evokes dark-romantic spellbooks, 19th-century alchemy journals, and eerie gothic storybooks. When rendered in Russian Cyrillic, its sharp serifs and deliberate dramatic strokes create an antique aura that contrasts sharply with the toxic neon accents. It lends confectionery titles ("Мармеладные Глазницы", "Тыквенный Трюфель с Перцем") an artisanal yet sinister weight.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`):** Grounded, neutral, and razor-sharp across low-resolution mobile screens. Used for checkout flows, nutrition values, weights, and product specifications.
- **Currency Styling:** The Kazakhstani Tenge symbol (`₸`) is paired directly with numerical values in bold weight. For product cards, numbers take `EB Garamond` while the `₸` sign scales at 85% cap-height to maintain optical balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for high-density, touch-centric mobile browsing with a 4-column fluid mobile grid expanding to an 8-column layout on tablets.

- **Canvas & Screen Margins:** Mobile devices use an edge safe-zone of `1rem` (`16px`), expanding to `1.5rem` (`24px`) on wider mobile viewports.
- **Card Gutters:** Product grids use a compact `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutter, letting high-contrast visuals sit side-by-side to replicate packed sweetshop shelves.
- **Vertical Cadence:** A strict 4px base scale governs vertical rhythm. Section dividers use generous breathing room (`2.5rem`) to separate chaotic visual treats into bite-sized, digestible chapters (e.g., "Зельеварение", "Хиты Ужаса", "Кислые Кошмары").

## Elevation & Depth

Depth does not rely on sterile grey drop-shadows. Instead, this system utilizes **Bioluminescent Volumetric Glows** and stacked obsidian glass layers:

1. **Base Abyssal Layer (Level 0):** Pure `#0D0D0D`. Non-interactive backgrounds, full-bleed canvas.
2. **Cauldron Vitrine (Level 1 - Product Cards & Tiles):** Surface `#141414` combined with a 1px structural stroke of `#2D1B4E`. On hover or touch, the border transitions to `#FF6B0033` with a subtle amber back-glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.12)`).
3. **Floating Reliquary (Level 2 - Sticky Navigation, Sheets, Drawers):** `#1A1A1AE6` backed by a heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Bound by a top rim highlight (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
4. **Spectral Modal / Cart Summary (Level 3):** Floats above a deep scrim (`rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.85)`). The surface is outlined with a dual-cast rim: an inner shadow of `rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.2)` and a soft outward pulse of `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances organic, jelly-like confection curves with structural digital framing. 

A baseline **Level 2 (Rounded)** approach provides `0.5rem` (`8px`) for standard internal elements (input fields, counters), scaling to `1rem` (`16px`) for cards and surface containers. Key conversion drivers—such as floating add-to-cart buttons, filter pills, and price badges—use expressive `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) or complete pill silhouettes. This pill geometry recalls candy drops, potion vials, and gelatin beans, dampening the harshness of the pitch-black void.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call to Terror / В корзину):** Saturated `#FF6B00` background, pure black `#0D0D0D` label text in `Inter` semi-bold, uppercase tracking. Border-radius `rounded-xl`. Enhanced with an ambient neon bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.45)`). Pressed state reduces scale to `0.97` and intensifies border halo.
- **Secondary Action (Детали, Состав):** Smoked translucent background (`#1A1A1A`), pure white text, 1px border colored `#2D1B4E`. Active state shifts the border to Acid Green (`#39FF14`).
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#FF6B00` text and an underline glow.

### Product Cards
- Built as dark vitrines. Background `#141414`, bordered with a hairline stroke of `#2D1B4E`.
- Card imagery uses an eerie gradient underlay: a radial burst of `#8A2BE222` behind dark candy images to elevate contrast against the pitch-black card.
- Prices are styled prominently: the amount in `EB Garamond` bold (`#FFFFFF`), paired with the Kazakhstani Tenge symbol (`#FF6B00`).

### Badges & Tags
- **Acid Slime Badge ("Острое", "Кислое", "Хит"):** Neon Acid Green fill at 12% opacity (`#39FF141F`), vibrant green text (`#39FF14`), 1px solid `#39FF1455` stroke, soft green ambient aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.2)`).
- **Curse Badge ("Лимитировано", "18+"):** Pumpkin Orange variant using identical micro-glow mechanics.
- All tags adopt pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) with `label-sm` typography.

### Input Fields & Search
- Inputs feature `#141414` background, `#A0A0A0` placeholder text, and a `#2D1B4E` resting border.
- On focus, the field ignites: border switches to `#8A2BE2`, accompanied by a smooth `0 0 12px rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.35)` outer halo. Clean sans-serif typing with zero lag.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Base container: `#141414` with `#2D1B4E` border.
- Selected state: Fills with `#39FF14`, presenting an obsidian checkmark or dot (`#0D0D0D`), surrounded by an electric acid-green halo.

### Specialized E-Commerce Components
- **Floating Witching Cart Bar:** Anchored to bottom mobile viewport (`safe-area-inset-bottom`). Smoked obsidian glass with a vivid `#FF6B00` quick-checkout button, item counter wrapped in `#39FF14`, and dynamic total displayed in Kazakhstani Tenge (`₸`).
- **Toxicity Meter (Sour / Spice Level):** 5-segment potion indicator inside product details, illuminating from `#8A2BE2` to `#39FF14` as intensity climbs.
